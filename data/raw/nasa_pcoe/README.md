# NASA battery archive

Restore `5. Battery Data Set.zip` here from the matching release data bundle, or obtain
item 5 from the [NASA PCoE data repository](https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/).

The raw archive is kept outside Git. Its provenance and checksum are recorded in
`data/data_manifest.csv` at the project root. See `docs/DATA_SETUP.md` for setup and
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for attribution.
